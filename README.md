# Privacy Policy — PDF Tools

**Effective date:** July 20, 2026
**App:** PDF Tools (`com.devcodeapps.pdftools`)
**Developer:** DevCodeApps
**Contact:** under.cntrl.dev@gmail.com

## Summary

PDF Tools processes all of your documents locally, on your device. Your files are never uploaded, scanned, or shared. The free version of the app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob; the optional one-time Pro purchase removes them. Ads are the only part of the app that uses the internet.

## Your documents

When you select a PDF or image file inside PDF Tools, the app uses Android's Storage Access Framework to read only the file(s) you explicitly choose. The app:

- Does not request broad storage permissions
- Does not scan, index, or inventory files on your device
- Only reads files you pick through the system picker

Files you process (merged PDFs, compressed PDFs, extracted images, etc.) are written to the app's private storage and made available via the system share sheet or save dialog. The app does not upload, copy, or transmit these files anywhere. **Your documents are never shared with any third party, including our advertising partner.**

## Data we collect

We — DevCodeApps — do not collect, store, or share any personal data ourselves. The app contains no analytics and no crash-reporting SDKs.

### Advertising (free version)

The free version shows interstitial ads served by **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

- Advertising ID (resettable device identifier)
- IP address and coarse device/network information
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This processing is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's advertising policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543). In regions where consent is required (e.g. EEA/UK under GDPR), the app shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time under **Settings → Ad privacy options**.

### In-app purchase (Pro)

The optional lifetime Pro upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by **Google Play Billing**. We never receive or store your payment details. Purchasing Pro removes all advertising from the app; with ads removed, no ad-related data is processed.

## Permissions

PDF Tools requests no runtime permissions. The advertising SDK adds the standard install-time entries `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, and `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`, which are used solely for serving ads in the free version.

## Children's privacy

PDF Tools is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Ads are served through Google AdMob in compliance with Google Play's Families policies.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted at the same URL with a revised effective date.
